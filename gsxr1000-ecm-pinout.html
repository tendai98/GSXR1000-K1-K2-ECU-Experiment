Suzuki GSX-R1000 K1/K2 ECM coupler pinout

Face-on map of the two ECM couplers (34-pin and 26-pin, 60 terminals), built from the 2001 GSX-R1000 service manual: FI system wiring diagram (p. 4-27 and section 11), the ECM coupler drawings in the troubleshooting charts (p. 4-36 to 4-59) and the full harness diagram.

Check the orientation before you probe or wire anything

  • The manual prints the terminal grid with the caption "ECM couplers" and does not say whether you are looking into the ECM's pins or into the harness plug. The two views are mirror images, so use the toggle and confirm with the checks below.
  • With the harness plug in hand: terminal 1 is the white/blue wire (ignition coil #1) at one end of a 9-way outer row of the larger plug. Along that row come B, Y, then the four gray injector wires, one empty cavity (8), and yellow/black (9) at the far end. On the smaller plug, terminal 35 is a black/white ground at the end of a 7-way outer row, next to a plain white wire (36).
  • On the bare ECM: terminals 18, 26, 27 and 35 are all grounds (E02, E03, E01, E1). They normally show continuity to each other inside the unit. 26 and 27 sit side by side at one end of the 9-way outer row opposite the terminal 1 row, with 18 directly above 26.

Reading notes

  • Row layout: larger coupler 1–9 / 10–17 / 18–25 / 26–34; smaller coupler 35–41 / 42–47 / 48–53 / 54–60. Confirmed against the highlighted terminals in the manual's charts (4–7, 9, 17, 36, 37, 42, 43, 44, 51, 54).
  • Wire colours: B black, Bl blue, Br brown, Dg dark green, G green, Gr gray, Lg light green, O orange, P pink, R red, W white, Y yellow. "Gr/W" means gray with a white tracer.
  • Terminal 47 (SM1B): the FI diagram prints Bl/G, the full harness diagram and the STV actuator coupler show B/Lg. Expect black/light-green on the bike.
  • Terminals 15, 16, 23, 24, 25 and 33 carry wires in the full harness diagram but are not named in the FI diagram. Their numbers come from their position in that drawing, so treat them as probable. 15/16/23/24/25 run to an unlabelled 7-way coupler drawn beside the ECM.
  • MS (21), TECH (56) and AT (60) are the manual's labels. The manual does not spell out their function, so the "connects to" text for those is my reading of the diagram.
  • Terminals shown hatched grey have no wire in any of the manual's diagrams.
